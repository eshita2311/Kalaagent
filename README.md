KalaAgent — AI Shopping Agent for Indian Artisan Handicrafts 

KalaAgent is an AI-powered shopping agent built exclusively to help India's artisans and craftspeople sell their handicrafts — to both human buyers and AI shopping agents. Built for Razorpay's AI Buildathon under the "AI Growth & Agentic Commerce" track.

THE PROBLEM
AI shopping agents (like ChatGPT or voice assistants) are starting to buy on our behalf — but they can't transact with most Indian artisan sellers, who lack structured product data, verifiable authenticity, and safe purchase flows for AI buyers.

WHAT KALAAGENT DOES
- AI-powered search with fully explainable reasoning for every match
- Honest decline handling — the AI refuses to guess when it can't confidently verify a product
- 7 handicraft categories: Painting, Pottery & Clay, Textile & Fiber, Basketry & Cane, Wood & Stone, Metal, and Paper Craft — 20+ real products with verified imagery and GI-tag provenance
- Bounded, server-capped AI upsell suggestions (the AI proposes, code enforces the limit)
- Real Razorpay test-mode checkout
- Full user accounts, wishlist, and order history
- Live analytics dashboard + complete audit trail for every AI decision
- 6-language support: English, Hindi, Tamil, Urdu, Bangla, Marathi
- Built-in help desk for buyer support
- Agent-readable catalog endpoint (schema.org structured data) so external AI shopping agents can discover and query products directly — real agent-to-agent commerce infrastructure, not just a chatbot on a store

TECH STACK
Frontend: React + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS
Backend: Node.js + Express + TypeScript
Database: SQLite
AI: LLM-powered matching, reasoning, and bounded upsell agent
Payments: Razorpay (Test Mode)
Auth: JWT + bcrypt

FUTURE SCOPE
Artisan self-onboarding with AI-assisted listing creation, deeper agent-to-agent payment protocol support, and a bounded negotiation agent reflecting how handicrafts are traditionally bought and sold in India.
